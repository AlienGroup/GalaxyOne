Privacy Policy

POPIA Compliant
This policy complies with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information for credit assessment:

  • Identity and contact details (name, ID number, address, phone, email)
  • Employment and income information
  • Bank account details for payments
  • Credit bureau reports (with your consent)

2. How We Use Your Information

  1. To assess your credit application as required by the NCA
  2. To verify your identity and prevent fraud
  3. To communicate about your account
  4. To comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Registered credit bureaus (TransUnion, Experian, XDS)
  • Payment processing partners (PayFast, Banks)
  • Regulatory authorities when required by law
  • Debt counselors if you apply for debt review

We will never sell your personal information.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures including:

  1. SSL encryption for all data transmissions
  2. Secure storage with access controls
  3. Regular security audits

5. Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal information we hold
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator
